# TF2 HUD Studio wiki

TF2 HUD Studio is a visual editor for Team Fortress 2 HUDs. Its preview follows the game's VGUI layout rules, so what
you see is where things end up in game.

## Start here
- **[[Getting Started]]**: download, first launch, opening or creating a HUD.
- **[[Installing and Sharing]]**: put your HUD in `tf/custom`, export a zip.

## Guides
- **[[Designer]]**: the canvas, selecting, dragging, snapping, the Inspector and the Elements tree.
- **[[Scheme]]**: colors, fonts, borders and custom font files.
- **[[Animations]]**: `hudanimations` events, the timeline and playback.
- **[[Problems]]**: what each warning means and how to fix it.
- **[[Editors]]**: the built-in source editor, and your own text and image editors.
- **[[Shortcuts]]**: every keyboard and mouse shortcut.

## Reference
- **[[How the Preview Works]]**: VGUI units, positions, pinning, `#base` and conditions.
- **[[Troubleshooting]]**: common problems and an FAQ.
- **[[Building]]**: building from source and running the tests.
- **[[Releases]]**: stable and cutting-edge builds, and how versions work.
