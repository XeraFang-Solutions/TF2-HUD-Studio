# Code of conduct

TF2 HUD Studio is made by people who like TF2 and like making things. We want the project to be a friendly place to
build HUDs, report bugs, review code and learn. That goes for everyone involved, whatever their experience or background.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most problems are misunderstandings. Ask before you assume.
- **Be patient with newcomers.** Everyone's first `.res` file was confusing. Explain things, link to the docs, and don't gatekeep.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** Reviews should be specific and constructive, and should say why.
- **Respect people's time and decisions.** Maintainers may say no to a feature. Contributors may not have time to make every change you ask for.
- **Give credit.** Say where code, art or HUD work came from, and respect other HUD authors' licenses.
- **Keep it on topic** in issues and pull requests. Trash talk belongs in the game.

## What's not OK

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or skill level.
- Sexual content, violent threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves.
- Doxxing: posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing on someone who has asked you to stop.
- Spam, advertising, or attempts to derail discussions.
- Posting other people's HUDs, assets or code against their license or without credit.

## Where this applies

This code covers the repository: issues, pull requests, discussions and the wiki. It also covers anywhere you
represent the project, such as a project account or an official channel.

## Reporting

If something here is broken, or someone makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately:
- through the **[XeraFang Solutions GitHub organization](https://github.com/XeraFang-Solutions)**, or
- for anything sensitive, through a private security advisory on this repository (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) with "Conduct" in the title.

Reports are handled confidentially, and we will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide what to do based on what happened and its impact. That may be:

1. a private note explaining the problem;
2. a public warning, or edited or hidden comments;
3. a temporary ban from the project's spaces;
4. a permanent ban, for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers who don't follow this code in good faith may be removed from their role.

*This code of conduct was written for TF2 HUD Studio. It draws on the ideas common to open-source community guidelines.*
